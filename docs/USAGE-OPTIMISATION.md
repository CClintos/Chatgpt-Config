# Usage optimisation

Measure verified deliverables and elapsed time together. Low allowance consumption alone does not establish efficiency, and higher consumption does not establish better work. After a meaningful task, record available model/effort, approximate allowance, retries, specialist calls, and the concrete verified outcome. Unavailable measurements should stay unknown; recording them must not delay the work.

When progress is slow, identify whether time is going into tool waits, repeated navigation, repeated investigation, reasoning, or approval loops. Change the method at that bottleneck. Research should settle a specific decision; implementation requests should end with completed changes and verification where feasible.

The 2026-10-02 workflow revision adapts the installed OpenAI Skill Creator guidance on outcome-based instructions and preserving task scope. Its scenarios are manual review criteria; no controlled before/after runtime speed improvement has yet been measured.

Compare the same task classes over several examples:

- Luna Medium versus High versus XHigh for routine and bounded hard work.
- Sol consultations where rework risk is material.
- Astra Low only when a consequential decision justifies explicit approval.

Do not infer model efficiency from a single task, a headline allowance percentage, or a successful plan without verified implementation. Revisit routing when the data shows a cheaper setting meets the quality bar or a short specialist call consistently prevents rework.
