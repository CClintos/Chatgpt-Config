# Car-audio orchestrator

Use this skill to route and structure car-audio work without duplicating a specialised Helix/REW methodology.

- Read `projects/car-audio/STATE.md` and the latest relevant `TUNING-LOG.md` entry first.
- Preserve measurement files, settings, microphone/calibration details, and known system state.
- Separate measured findings, calculated results, and subjective listening impressions.
- Prefer deterministic analysis for numeric work and one controlled change at a time.
- Defer detailed Helix/REW procedure to an installed specialist skill when available; do not recreate it here.
- Escalate reasoning for difficult interpretation or conflicting measurements, not for repetitive calculations or missing permissions.
- Record the change, expected effect, result, and rollback in the tuning log. Never overwrite a known-good DSP state without preserving it.

Current reference system: VW Golf Mk7 2013 TSI Highline RHD; Helix P SIX DSP MK2; Alpine XA90M; German Maestro MS6508 front; Morel Maximo rear differential fill; Brax Matrix 10.1 sealed sub; UMIK-1 and REW; sound-quality target. Treat these as defaults, not proof that a future vehicle or configuration is unchanged.
